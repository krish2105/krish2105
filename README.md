## What I build

I build machine learning systems that sit inside a business decision rather than next to it. The pattern is usually the same. Take operational data that nobody has cleaned, train a model that is honest about what it does not know, then wrap it in an interface where a non-technical reviewer can approve, edit or reject what the model proposed.

Three lines of work:

- **Fraud and risk models** for payments and lending, where a false negative costs money and a false positive costs a customer.
- **Multilingual NLP for customer operations**, where the same complaint arrives in English, Arabic, Hindi and Tagalog and still has to be triaged consistently.
- **Forecasting and decision dashboards** for retail and finance teams who need a number they can defend in a room full of people who did not build the model.

Every system I ship keeps a human in the approval path and writes an audit trail. No auto-send, no black box scoring a customer without a reason attached.

## Who I build it for

- **Banks and fintechs** that need churn, fraud and retention scoring they can explain to a regulator, not just to a data scientist.
- **Retail and supply chain teams** who want demand forecasts tied to a reorder decision instead of a chart.
- **Finance and strategy functions** that need models translated back into a number, a recommendation and a stated confidence level.
- **Course and research teams** in the GCC building multilingual, Gulf-specific systems where off-the-shelf English-only tooling breaks.

Based in Dubai, currently completing a Master of AI in Business at SP Jain School of Global Management.
